## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-30

### Added

- Experimental local-only React evidence-review preview.
- Deterministic programmatic fixed-workstation packing fixture.
- Manual zones, low-level observations, immutable corrections, and restore history.
- Coverage, zone-fingerprint, source-identity, comparability, observability, and guardrail gates.
- Guided synthetic 3 Before + 3 After descriptive comparison.
- User-authored reversible change and keep/revert/uncertain decision bound to current evidence.
- Desktop and mobile review experiences with keyboard, reduced-motion, forced-colors, and axe coverage.
- Local-media decode lifecycle fixture and removable no-model adapter.
- Production CSP/no-egress and public-release scans.

### Documentation

- Public architecture, privacy, fixture, limitations, testing, and decision records.
- Contribution, conduct, security, support, and issue/PR templates.

### Evidence boundary

- The release is a synthetic/manual portfolio preview. It does not validate product need, real-video reliability, causal improvement, privacy compliance, or production readiness.
